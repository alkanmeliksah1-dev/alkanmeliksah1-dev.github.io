Biography

About

Melikşah Alkan (b. 2005) is an Istanbul-based composer, flutist and sound designer. He began his formal music education at the age of fifteen and is currently studying composition at İstanbul Topkapı University with Uğurcan Öztekin and Emre Dündar, and electroacoustic music with Furkan Keçeli.

His music draws on both classical and contemporary Western techniques. His works have been performed by Ensemble Intercontemporain at the Bilgi New Music Festival and by the Semplice Quartet at Atatürk Cultural Center (AKM) as part of the Sesin Yolculuğu Young Composers Festival. He received an honorable mention at the Gilgamesh International Composition Competition.

He has attended masterclasses with Pierluigi Billone, Enis Gümüş and Mehmet Ali Uzunselvi. Alongside concert music, he composes and designs sound for theatre productions.
